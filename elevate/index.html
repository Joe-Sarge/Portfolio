Unsung Hoopers Society: Margo Dydek

Unsung Hoopers is my series on players the game forgot. This episode tells the story of Margo Dydek, the tallest player in WNBA history.

The goal is to make a stranger care about a story they never knew. The numbers show it worked: almost all of the reach came from people who did not follow the account.

Atlanta Dream: Sneakerhead Night

The Dream's Sneakerhead Night had lost its impact. The team needed signature games that sold tickets and made content for potential new fans.

I wrote a Theme Nights Playbook first, then built the night from the front door to the final buzzer around Atlanta's sneaker culture. A new sponsor, Cash App, added incremental dollars after seeing the plan.

Golf · Short-form

Break 100 With Me

I have played golf for about five years, and I love the game though I'm still not a great player. This fall I set a public goal: break 100 by Halloween or play golf in a costume, and film every step.

Each episode puts the round or drill on screen for fans of golf to follow along. It is a small, honest test of a simple idea: the struggle to get better is a story that brings people into the game.
